### 1. Overview
ViveScript Solutions LLC operates the **CalorieSnap** mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how CalorieSnap handles information when you use our application.

At ViveScript Solutions LLC, user privacy is our foundational principle. CalorieSnap is designed as a focused calorie estimation utility. **We do not require user accounts, we do not require logins, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as names, passwords, or emails.**

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, ages, weights, or user profile records.
- **No Location Data:** CalorieSnap does not request or access GPS, coarse location, or network-derived location data.
- **No Camera, Microphone, or Media Access:** The application operates without access to your device camera, audio, or private photo library.

---

### 3. Local Storage & Offline Operation
- **Offline Food Database:** The food items, calorie values, and serving portion models are bundled locally inside the application binary.
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** Your searches, selected portion sizes, and calorie calculations occur entirely in temporary volatile device memory (RAM) and are not sent to any proprietary cloud server or database.

---

### 4. Sharing Feature
CalorieSnap provides a one-tap **Share** button. When you tap Share:
- The app uses the standard Android operating system share sheet (`ACTION_SEND`).
- The plain text summary (containing the food name, portion, and estimated calories) is transferred directly to the third-party application you explicitly select (such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, or Messaging).
- CalorieSnap does not intercept, log, or monitor what is shared or to whom it is delivered.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)
CalorieSnap integrates the official **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK** (`com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads`) to support free utility features:
- **Advertising Data:** Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically process non-personal device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, coarse network telemetry, and app interaction data for the purposes of serving advertisements, frequency capping, and preventing fraudulent ad traffic.
- **Privacy Controls:** Users can manage and reset their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through their Android device system settings (*Settings > Google > Ads*).
- **Google Privacy Policy:** For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Analytics & Crash Reports:** No standalone third-party behavioral analytics or commercial tracking trackers are used.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
CalorieSnap is designed for general audiences. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 7. Medical Disclaimer
CalorieSnap provides general nutritional estimates for informational and educational convenience only. Nutritional content, recipes, cooking techniques, and portion sizes vary significantly. CalorieSnap does not provide medical, dietary, or clinical advice.
