### Full Description (Play Store Compliant, Markdown / Plain Text)

CalorieSnap is the quick, simple, and offline food calorie estimator designed for everyday meals. Whether you are enjoying a home-cooked plate of rice and dal, dining out on chicken biryani, or grabbing an afternoon street snack, CalorieSnap helps you estimate calories in seconds—with no login, no complex diet logs, and no internet connection required.

🥗 KEY FEATURES:

• Comprehensive South Asian & Global Food Database
Search from a curated collection of everyday Bangladeshi, Indian, and South Asian favorites alongside international foods. From steamed white rice (sada bhat), atta roti, paratha, bhuna khichuri, and chicken biryani to hilsa fish curry, dim bhuna, aloo bhaji, singara, fuchka, mishti doi, dudh cha, fruits, and burgers—all available offline.

• Instant Serving Size & Portion Adjustments
Choose from standard serving portions (1 cup, 1 plate, 1 piece, small bowl) or use the quick quantity multiplier (0.5x, 1x, 2x, etc.). Prefer exact weights? Switch to custom grams mode to calculate calories for any specific portion.

• Instant Nutrition & Macro Breakdown
View estimated calories (kcal) along with approximate carbohydrates, protein, and fat content calculated in real time as you adjust your portions.

• One-Tap Instant Social Sharing
Easily share clean, formatted calorie summaries with friends, family, or fitness buddies across WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram Stories, TikTok, or messaging apps with a single tap.

• Dual Language (English & বাংলা)
Switch between English and Bengali (বাংলা) food names and descriptions instantly with one tap.

• 100% Offline & Private
No account needed. No passwords or sign-ups. CalorieSnap works completely on your device without transmitting your food lookups to remote servers.
